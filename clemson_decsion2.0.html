        About this model & opponent setup

        Imported defensive data: Clemson, LSU, and Georgia Tech tables are pulled straight from down & distance analysis reports and represent what each team's defense allows (run %, plays faced, and yards/play surrendered) by situation.

        Sample filter: Every table excludes plays against FCS opponents and 2nd-half plays where win probability was above 95% or below 5% (garbage time), so the numbers reflect competitive, representative snaps rather than blowout or cupcake-game outliers. All imported tables are treated as already reflecting this filter from their source reports.

        Imported defensive data: Every 2026 opponent now has a full-season down & distance defensive report imported directly from real 2025 regular-season data — no modeled or estimated tables remain. All rows are tagged Imported Report Data.

        Colors: Table headers, total rows, and the 4th-down end zone dynamically re-skin to the selected team's official primary/secondary colors.

        EPA model: The 4th-down Go/FG/Punt recommendation blends baseline Expected Points curves with a continuous game-leverage multiplier (score margin × quarter × time remaining) and an opponent defensive-tendency factor (how many yards/play that defense surrenders on the current down & distance vs. league average) — see the About panel in the Sideline Card write-up for the full formula.
